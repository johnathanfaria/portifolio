Data Sources & Integration Pipeline

The foundation: mapping and orchestrating all critical data streams—IoT sensors (traffic, environmental), citizen platforms, public transport, and utilities—via a flexible, secure ELT pipeline. Integration highlights: real-time ingestion (MQTT, Kafka), batch loads (Airflow/ADF), cloud storage (Azure Data Lake), all under GDPR-compliant design.

Figure 1: Robust ELT pipeline: from diverse urban sources to a unified, actionable city data lake and ML analytics.
Data Modeling for City Scale

I implemented a hybrid data modeling approach, leveraging time-series DBs for sensor data (speed, count, incident events) and relational models for reference and compliance. Benefit: Ultra-fast analytics without sacrificing integrity, lineage, or data minimization mandates.

Figure 2: Seamless integration of real-time streams with master data for powerful, auditable analytics.
Metadata & Data Cataloguing

I led the rollout of automated technical and business metadata harvesting, inspired by modern DataHub approaches. Features: End-to-end data lineage, operational and business context, automated validation, GDPR and audit traceability.

Figure 3: Automated and steward-driven metadata lifecycle: harvesting, enrichment, validation, publishing, and monitoring.
Master Data Management (MDM)

To eliminate duplication and support cross-departmental collaboration, I architected a hub-and-spoke MDM system—building “golden records” for every citizen, vehicle, and sensor. Inspired by global best practices (e.g. Barcelona): combining fuzzy matching, survivorship, and real-time sync to city apps.

Figure 4: MDM hub: linking city, vehicle, and IoT data for a single, trusted citywide data foundation.
Quality, Compliance & Governance

I established automated data quality monitoring (accuracy, completeness, timeliness, consistency), and implemented full GDPR and AI ethics (IBM AI Fairness 360) frameworks. Outcome: Compliance “by design” plus ongoing dashboards and alerts for city data stewards.

Analytics, ML, & Real-World Results

I designed analytics workflows for real-time traffic/safety dashboards, predictive ML models (e.g. incident risk, parking optimization), and prescriptive interventions. Result: Actionable insights in the hands of city management, powering smarter, safer, and more responsive urban living.
